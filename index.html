Your Training Max is about 90% of your true 1-rep max. Every weight is calculated from these — main sets from the main lift's TM, and BBB volume from the volume lift's own TM.
Schedule
Same four days either way. On a 3-day week they roll Mon/Wed/Fri, so a cycle takes ~5 weeks — position is tracked by sessions completed, not the calendar.
Assistance Targets
Rep totals per session, set per training day. A 0 means the category is intentionally skipped that day — the reason shows on the card. Raise targets (or add load) to progress.
In-Season Mode
Halves every non-zero assistance target, drops BBB to 3×10, and prefers the 3-day schedule. Copenhagen planks are unaffected.
Bar & Plates
